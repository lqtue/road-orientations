How to read

Each petal = a compass bearing. Longer = more road length that way. Both modes are cumulative: each ring includes all roads from the center outward. Radii: colors are distance bands. Detail: colors are road types — toggle in sidebar. Hover chart for values; hover a map ring to isolate that band.
